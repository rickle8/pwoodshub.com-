Every pick is scored by what the player produced for the owner who drafted him, bench weeks included, so it grades the pick and not the lineup calls. Each pick is compared with players at the same position taken around the same spot ("par"). That way a 14th-rounder isn't punished for not being a first-rounder, and quarterbacks don't all look like steals just because they score the most points. Kickers and defenses aren't graded, since everyone streams them.
