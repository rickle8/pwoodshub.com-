Trade Finder

Every one-for-one swap between every pair of teams, keeping only the ones where both rosters end up projecting more points from the lineup they can actually start. That isn't a free lunch — it happens when two teams have opposite surpluses, like a manager with three startable tight ends and a hole at running back. Deals are filtered to a roughly even exchange of trade value, so the other owner has some reason to say yes. Refreshed hourly.
