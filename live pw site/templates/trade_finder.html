Trade Finder

Every 1-for-1 and 2-for-1 between every pair of teams, keeping only deals where both teams' best possible starting lineup projects to score more. That happens when two teams have opposite needs, like one team with three good tight ends and another with none. Deals are also kept roughly even on trade value, so the other owner has a reason to say yes. In a 2-for-1, the team getting the single best player has to pay a little extra in total value.
